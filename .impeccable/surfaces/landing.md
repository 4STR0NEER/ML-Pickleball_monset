# Landing page surface brief

Scope: marketing landing page (Persuade mode) for ML Lifestyle Park's booking website.
Audience: prospective customers deciding whether to book a basketball court, futsal field, or pickleball court, arriving from social/search instead of Facebook Messenger.
Job: understand what the facility offers, trust it's legitimate and bookable online, and reach the "Check Availability" action.
Proof/content: real facility name, real sport/court counts (3 basketball, 2 futsal, 9 pickleball), real hours (4PM-12AM PH time daily), real booking model (instant confirm on payment, no approval wait). No real photos/video/testimonials yet.
Constraints: hero must use a placeholder for the 3D video (labeled as such); no invented pricing, testimonials, or claims; must stay legible/functional per craft floor even while bold.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is built as a barangay court's tarpaulin bulletin wall at night, not a generic booking-SaaS landing page — bold hand-set poster type, printed grain and staple/tape marks, floodlight glow — refusing both the clean-SaaS-hero default and its opposite (a flat text-only schedule form).

OWN-WORLD: Navy-ink-on-cream poster stock as the page ground; green as the single "available/go" accent lifted from the logo's leaf; a warm floodlight-amber as the secondary accent marking the hero's night transition; heavy condensed display caps (Anton/Archivo Black register) set like hand-painted court signage for headlines, a workhorse humanist sans (Work Sans) for body and the booking UI; halftone/print-grain texture used decoratively on section dividers and cards, never on functional booking controls.

STORY: A visitor lands, immediately reads "this is a real, active neighborhood sports court, and I can book it right now online" from the hero, sees the three sports and real hours, previews how availability selection works, and reaches Check Availability without needing to message anyone.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed hero (placeholder 3D video treatment) of the courts moving from golden late-afternoon light into floodlit night; the ML Lifestyle Park wordmark and a bulletin-style headline overlaid upper-left like a strung banner; a bold "Check Availability" primary CTA in the lower third; a live-feeling mini slot-grid strip (green/booked tiles) anchoring the bottom edge as proof of the real mechanic.

FORM: community tarpaulin/bulletin-poster culture (own grounded candidate, direction concept-seed key 066b328a, assigned index 7), raised by: (1) non-color-dependent availability states donated from the emission-line-rail challenger (pattern/icon alongside green/red, not color alone); (2) the dusk-to-floodlight hero time-arc donated from the rain-night-cityscape challenger, matching the real 4PM-12AM operating window; (3) full commitment to print/ink texture donated from the silkscreen-loft challenger (grain and registration marks as decorative flourish only, never on functional UI).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Color strategy

Full palette (3-4 named roles): Navy (ink/headlines/nav), Cream (page ground/paper), Green (primary CTA + "available" signal, from logo), Amber (floodlight/night accent). Chosen over Restrained because this is a Persuade surface and the brand already pins an active, energetic identity (leaping figure logo); chosen over Drenched because the booking UI itself (Operate-mode component embedded in this page) needs the neutral cream/navy base to stay legible.
